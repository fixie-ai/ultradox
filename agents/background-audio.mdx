Background audio plays an audio clip behind the agent's voice for the duration of a call. A little ambient noise, such as a busy
office or a humming air conditioner, can make an agent sound like it's somewhere real rather than in a silent studio.

The clip loops for the whole call. It plays under the agent's speech and continues while nobody is talking.

## Audio Clips

Background audio comes from an audio clip. Ultravox provides built-in clips, such as `office`, and you can upload up to 5 of your
own. Clips you upload are private to your account.

An uploaded clip must be:
* An uncompressed (PCM) WAV file
* Mono, with 16-bit samples
* Sampled between 16kHz and 48kHz
* At most 60 seconds long, and at most 6MB

Ultravox keeps only the audio and its format from the file you upload, dropping other metadata. A clip's audio can't be
changed after upload. To replace it, delete the clip and upload a new one.

### In the Web App

The [Audio Clips](https://app.ultravox.ai/audio-clips) page in the Ultravox web app lists your clips and the built-in ones. You can
play any of them, upload a new clip, or delete one of yours.

### With the API

Upload a clip with [Create Audio Clip](/api-reference/audio-clips/audio-clips-post). Its name must be unique within your account:

```bash
curl --request POST https://api.ultravox.ai/api/audio_clips \
  --header 'X-API-Key: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --form 'file=@"/path/to/my_office.wav"' \
  --form 'name=my_office' \
  --form 'description=Background chatter recorded in our office'
```

[List Audio Clips](/api-reference/audio-clips/audio-clips-list) returns your clips and the built-in ones, newest first, and
[Get Audio Clip Audio](/api-reference/audio-clips/audio-clips-audio-get) downloads a clip's audio.

## Adding Background Audio to an Agent

In the [agent builder](https://app.ultravox.ai/agents), open the settings panel and choose a clip under **Background Audio**. You
can preview the clip there and adjust its volume.

With the API, set `backgroundAudio` in the agent's `callTemplate`, naming the clip by its name or ID:

```js
{
  "name": "front_desk",
  "callTemplate": {
    "systemPrompt": "You are a friendly receptionist...",
    "backgroundAudio": {
      "audioClip": "office",
      "volume": 0.3
    }
  }
}
```

`volume` multiplies the clip's samples, from 0 to 10. 1 plays the clip as recorded, and the default of 0.3 plays it at 30% of
its recorded amplitude. Values above 1 amplify the clip, and any sample pushed past the maximum level is clipped, which distorts
the sound.

Ultravox stores the clip's ID, so renaming a clip doesn't affect the agents that use it.

## Calls

Calls created with an agent use the agent's background audio. To change it for a single call, set `backgroundAudio` when
[creating the call](/agents/making-calls#overriding-agent-settings). The call's value replaces the agent's.

Calls created directly, without an agent, take the same `backgroundAudio` field in the
[Create Call](/api-reference/calls/calls-post) request.

Background audio needs a voice call. Creating an agent or call that sets `backgroundAudio` returns a 400 error when its
[medium restriction](/agents/making-calls#settings-that-require-voice) doesn't allow voice.

## Deleting a Clip

Deleting a clip you uploaded also removes it from the agents that use it, and their new calls play no background audio. Choose a
different clip for those agents if you still want one.
